MS DHONI — Career Timeline

BIRTH & FAMILY

MS Dhoni was born on July 7, 1981, in Ranchi, Bihar (present-day Jharkhand) in a Hindu Rajput family to Pan Singh and Devki Devi. His paternal village is in the Lamgara block in Almora, Uttarakhand. Dhoni has a sister and a brother — Jayanti Gupta and Narendra Singh Dhoni.

SCHOOLING

Dhoni did his schooling at DAV Jawahar Vidya Mandir, Ranchi, and excelled in badminton, football, and cricket. He played as a goalkeeper for his football team and for a local cricket club.

1998

Dhoni was selected for the Central Coal Fields Limited (CCL) team. His excellent performance with CCL earned him a place in Bihar's Ranji Trophy squad.

2002–2003

Playing for Jharkhand in the Ranji Trophy and Deodhar Trophy, Dhoni gained recognition for his lower-order contributions and hard-hitting batting style.

2004

Selected for India A, Dhoni toured Zimbabwe and Kenya. He scored 362 runs in 6 innings at an average of 72.40, turning heads with his explosive batting and sharp glovework.

2007

Named vice-captain for ODIs against South Africa and England. In September 2007, Dhoni was elected captain of the Indian T20 team and led India to win the inaugural ICC World Twenty20.

2009

Dhoni scored 124 off 107 balls vs Australia. He topped the ICC ODI Batsman rankings and took his first international wicket against West Indies in the Champions Trophy.

2011–2012

Led India to the 2011 Cricket World Cup title, finishing the final with an iconic six scoring 91* against Sri Lanka, winning Man of the Match. India became the first team to win the World Cup on home soil.

2013

India won the ICC Champions Trophy. Dhoni became the first and only captain in cricket history to win all three major ICC trophies — World T20, World Cup, and Champions Trophy.

2017

Stepped down as captain of the Indian limited-overs team. Was named wicketkeeper in the ICC 'Team of the Tournament' at the 2017 Champions Trophy.

2019

Selected for the 2019 Cricket World Cup. In the semi-final against New Zealand, Dhoni scored a half-century but was controversially run out at a crucial stage, ending India's campaign.

PERSONAL LIFE

Dhoni married Sakshi Singh Rawat on July 4, 2010. The couple has a daughter, Ziva, born on February 6, 2015. He is known for his calm demeanour both on and off the field.

PLAYING STYLE

A right-handed batsman and wicket-keeper, Dhoni is famous for his Helicopter Shot, his calm finishing under pressure, and lightning-quick stumpings. He holds the world record for most stumpings by a wicketkeeper.

AWARDS

Padma Bhushan (2018) • Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna • Padma Shri (2009) • ICC World Test XI (2009, 2010, 2013)

VIRAT KOHLI — Career Timeline

2002

Kohli made his debut with the Delhi Under-15 team in the 2002-03 Polly Umrigar Trophy, captaining the team for the next edition. He was later selected for the Delhi Under-17 team and finished as the highest run-scorer in the Vijay Merchant Trophy.

2008

A life-changing year: Kohli captained the Under-19 Cricket World Cup-winning team, was bought by RCB for $30,000 in the first IPL auction, and made his international debut for India.

2010

With Tendulkar rested, Kohli played every match in the tri-nation ODI tournament in Bangladesh. His phenomenal performance earned praise from captain MS Dhoni.

2011

Kohli was part of the 2011 World Cup-winning team and became the first Indian batsman to score a century on World Cup debut.

2015

Kohli became the first Indian batsman to score a century against Pakistan in a World Cup match in 2015. He also became Test vice-captain around this period.

FAMILY

Started dating Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in 2013, earning the nickname "Virushka." The couple married in an intimate ceremony on December 11, 2017 in Florence, Italy, and welcomed a daughter named Vamika.

AWARDS

Arjuna Award (2013) • Padma Shri (2017) • Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (2018) • ICC ODI Player of the Year (2012) • ICC Test Player of the Year (2018)

CAPTAINCY

India lost the 2021 ICC World Test Championship Final to New Zealand — Kohli's third defeat as captain in ICC knockouts. India also failed to reach the T20 World Cup 2021 semi-finals under his captaincy. He captained RCB for 8 IPL seasons but couldn't win a trophy.

RISHABH PANT — Career Timeline

BIRTH & FAMILY

Born on October 4, 1997, in Haridwar, Uttarakhand. His family moved to Roorkee and his father Rajendra Pant nurtured his cricketing passion. Pant trained at Sonnet Cricket Club in Delhi, where coaches spotted his enormous potential.

DOMESTIC CRICKET

His aggressive batting quickly made him a standout in domestic cricket. Representing Delhi, he announced himself with a blistering triple century in the Ranji Trophy against Maharashtra in 2016-17, earning him an India T20 call-up.

IPL DEBUT

Pant's IPL journey began with the Delhi Daredevils (now Delhi Capitals) in 2016. His explosive batting and agile wicketkeeping made him indispensable, and he grew into one of the team's most important players.

INTERNATIONAL DEBUT

Made his T20I debut against England in 2017, followed by Test and ODI debuts. Despite critics pointing out wicketkeeping lapses, Pant showed rapid learning and continually refined his game.

TEST CRICKET

His defining moment came in the 2020-21 Border-Gavaskar Trophy in Australia. An unbeaten 89 at the Gabba helped India pull off a historic series win, silencing critics and cementing his legacy.

RECORDS

Pant is the fastest Indian wicketkeeper to reach 1000 Test runs. His penchant for sixes and match-winning knocks have made him one of the most exciting young talents in world cricket.

BEYOND CRICKET

Off the field, Pant's amiable personality and social media presence have endeared him to millions of fans globally. He is known for his camaraderie and lighthearted spirit within the team.
